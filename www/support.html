Guitar Lick Speed Trainer - Support

Thank you for using Guitar Lick Speed Trainer! This page covers common questions and how to get help.

Getting Started

Guitar Lick Speed Trainer helps you build speed by looping licks at increasing tempos. Select a preset or create your own, set your tempo, and hit play.

Common Questions

No sound?

  • iOS: Make sure your device is not on silent mode, or check that the volume is turned up.
  • Android: Ensure media volume (not just ringer) is turned up.
  • Tap the play button once to start — the first tap initialises audio on mobile devices.

How do I create my own lick?

  • Tap the Edit button to enter edit mode.
  • Use the note palette to add notes to the bar.
  • Tap a note cell to set the fret/pitch and bend.
  • Tap Save when done.

How does speed-up work?

Set the BPM increase and number of bars between increases. The app will automatically raise the tempo as you play, helping you gradually build speed.

Privacy

Guitar Lick Speed Trainer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your presets and settings are stored locally on your device only.
